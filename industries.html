Industries

One Agricultural Operating System. Built for every part of the agricultural ecosystem.

Agriculture is incredibly diverse. The operational challenges facing an independent commercial farmer are very different from those of a multinational agricultural business, a government agricultural programme, a financial institution financing agriculture or an agronomy business supporting farmers.

Yet they all rely on the same foundation. Connected operations. Reliable information. Better decisions. FarmX adapts to each of these environments while remaining one connected Agricultural Operating System.

Commercial Farms

Helping farmers maximise the profit that is possible.

Commercial farming has become increasingly complex.

Margins are tighter.

Labour is more difficult to manage.

Input costs continue to rise.

Managers are expected to make hundreds of operational decisions every day.

FarmX gives commercial farming businesses complete operational visibility across labour, machinery, irrigation, crops, stores, fuel, finance and daily operations.

Instead of chasing information, managers spend more time making decisions.

Instead of disconnected departments, the whole farm operates as one connected business.

FarmX helps commercial farms improve accountability, increase operational discipline, reduce waste and ultimately maximise the profit that is possible from every hectare.
