Leadership

The team building the Agricultural Operating System.

FarmX is being built by a small team combining farming experience, technical execution and product thinking. The leadership team brings together operational agriculture, software engineering, customer understanding and the belief that modern farms need better systems for better decisions.

AG

Adam Gordon-Strong

Founder & Managing Director

Adam is the founder of FarmX and the farming operator behind the vision. His experience spans commercial agriculture, farm management, operational systems and technology. FarmX was born from the belief that farming has become too complex for one person to optimise alone, and that AI can now help farms make better decisions.
